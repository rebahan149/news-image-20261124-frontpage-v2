## Hero stories

**1 — EUROPE · Drone war: 699 downed, Poland on alert.**
Russia's Defence Ministry said its air defences downed 699
Ukrainian drones overnight, 355 of them headed for the Moscow
region and 63 destroyed on approach to the capital; flight
restrictions were imposed at two Moscow airports (AFP, Tue).
Ukraine said a Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia killed 2 and
injured more than 40 (Anadolu, Tue). A Russian drone briefly
entered Polish airspace overnight (Reuters, Tue); NATO's AWACS
forward deployment to Poland was confirmed Mon 23 Nov, so the
page phrases it as a deployment "sent", not a Tuesday decision.

**2 — MIDDLE EAST · Baghdad seizes Kurdish-held oilfields.**
Reuters (Baghdad, Nov 24): PM Ali al-Zaidi ordered the federal
oil ministry to manage oilfields under Kurdish security control
in Diyala, Kirkuk, Salahuddin and Nineveh, the border authority
to take over all Kurdistan-region crossings, and KRG exports to
move only through the federal ministry, following a North Oil
Company smuggling probe. Kurdish PM Masrour Barzani rejected it.

**3 — ASIA · Malaysia to send 6,500 Rohingya back.**
AFP via Al Jazeera (Tue): Anwar Ibrahim told parliament Malaysia
will return more than 6,500 Myanmar nationals (mostly Rohingya)
in December — 1,500 on three navy vessels on Dec 6–7 and 5,303
by air at Myanmar's expense — five days after the ICJ ruled
Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya.

## Numbered panels 4–11

**4 — MIDDLE EAST · Iran: tankers hit its mines.** IRGC Navy
said three oil tankers struck naval mines and caught fire in
the Strait of Hormuz (IRNA, early Tue 24 Nov; Tasnim said the
statement was issued Monday night). Printed as a claim:
unverified, and US CENTCOM had not responded.

**5 — MIDDLE EAST · Strikes kill 7; meningitis warning.**
Al Jazeera (Tue): Israeli strikes killed at least 7, including
a child and three police officers (al-Mawasi and Wadi Gaza);
Gaza's Health Ministry warned of a meningitis epidemic — 272
cases in October, more than 8× the normal monthly rate.

**6 — EUROPE · EU caps Chinese hybrid cars.** Reuters
(Brussels, Nov 24): the EU imposed temporary import limits on
hybrid vehicles, announced in the official journal Tuesday —
China capped at 350,000 cars a year (about half this year's
expected arrivals), applying from Dec 1, provisional, max 200
days.

**7 — ASIA · Chip stocks lift Asia.** Reuters (Hong Kong,
Nov 24): KOSPI surged more than 7%, Taiex +9.1% rebounding from
an eight-month low, Nikkei about +4%, Hang Seng +0.2%, in the
rebound after Nvidia's Monday surge.

**8 — ASIA · Restaurant gas blast kills six.** Reuters/AP
(Tue): a suspected gas explosion at a hot pot eatery on Nguyen
Van Tuyet St, Dong Da, Hanoi, at about 08:00 local killed 6 and
injured 10 (Reuters figure).

**9 — ASIA · Hasina says she will fly home 16 Dec.** Reuters/AP
(Tue): Sheikh Hasina's spokesperson said Tuesday she plans to
return from exile in India on 16 December (Victory Day) and
asked to be flown on Biman; the government says she can return
only by extradition. She was sentenced to death in absentia.

**10 — OCEANIA · Solomons swaps China pact for Australia.**
Reuters (Sydney, Nov 24, reporting the Sydney Morning Herald):
Honiara has told China it will not renew the 2022 security pact
when it expires in April; PM Matthew Wale signs a new
comprehensive treaty with PM Albanese in Canberra on Wednesday.

**11 — AMERICAS · Venezuela lays its debt bare.** Reuters
(Nov 24): Caracas published its macroeconomic framework on
Tuesday — the first full picture in years — putting debts at
$237.5bn and projecting the economy to more than double in five
years to $200.9bn, a step toward a giant restructuring.
